iBoot32Patcher, built from LukeZGD's fork of iBoot32Patcher at commit
1ff9bd14648efae691ed23ae0abb55a4635111e3 (https://github.com/LukeZGD/iBoot32Patcher), with
build-support/patches/iBoot32Patcher-ltm.patch applied. The archive and its SHA-256 are pinned in
build-support/dependencies.json.

firmwarekit runs it as a separate process for the iPad 1 (k48ap) recipe: it patches an iBoot image
with the RSA/personalization bypass, the debug-enable patch and the boot-args string, and firmwarekit
then seals the result into the NOR it builds.

file, and the complete corresponding source is the pinned archive above with the patch named here.
